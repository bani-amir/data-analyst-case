# Case study

A communications stakeholder has provided this social media dataset and asked:
"What is driving engagement, and what should we do differently based on the data?"
You have approximately 15 minutes to explore the data and prepare your response.
You may use Python, an AI coding agent, Git, the terminal ,and any available libraries.

At the end, please present:

1. Your most important findings
2. Any limitations or concerns
3. One or two recommendations for a non-technical comms leader

Please think aloud as you work. I'm evaluating your approach, not how much analysis you complete.

##  Files

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `social_media_sentiment_raw.csv` | Raw, uncleaned dataset (2,260 rows, 13 columns) |

##  Dataset

- **Records:** 2,260 posts (before cleaning)
- **Platforms:** Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn
- **Countries:** 12 countries covered
- **Time range:** January 2023 – December 2024
- **Columns:** `post_id`, `timestamp`, `platform`, `user`, `followers_count`, `verified`, `text`, `hashtags`, `country`, `likes`, `retweets_shares`, `comments`, `sentiment`
